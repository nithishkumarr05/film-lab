Common Questions

Film FAQs

What's the difference between C-41 and E-6?

C-41 is for color negative film (Portra, Pro 400H). E-6 is for slide/transparency film (Velvia, Provia).

Can I push/pull my film?

Yes! We offer push/pull processing for both C-41 and B&W films. Just note your request when dropping off.

How should I store my film?

Store unexposed film in a cool, dry place. Refrigerate for long-term storage. Exposed film should be developed within 6 months.

Do you offer scanning with development?

Absolutely! We offer high-resolution scans with all film processing orders.
