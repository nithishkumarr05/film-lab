Our Story

Preserving the Art of
Analog Photography

Since 2012, Film Lab has been the sanctuary for photographers who believe in the magic of silver halide crystals, chemical baths, and the tactile beauty of handcrafted prints.

Our Philosophy

More Than Just
Chemistry

In an era of instant gratification, we champion the slow, deliberate craft of film photography. Every roll that passes through our hands is treated with reverence — from the moment it's loaded onto our dip-and-dunk processors to the final archival wash.

Our master printers have decades of combined experience, having studied under legends of the silver gelatin tradition. We don't just process film — we preserve memories, one grain at a time.
