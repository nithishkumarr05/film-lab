Common Questions

Service FAQs

What's the turnaround time?

Standard processing takes 3-5 business days. Rush options available (24-48 hours) for an additional fee.

Do you offer push/pull processing?

Yes! We offer push/pull processing for B&W and C-41 films. Just note your request when dropping off.

What film formats do you accept?

We process 35mm, 120 (medium format), 4x5, and 8x10 sheet film. 110 and 126 formats also accepted.

How will I receive my scans?

Scans are delivered via Google Drive or WeTransfer. USB drives available upon request.
