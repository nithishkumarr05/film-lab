Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services.

How do I mail in my film?

Visit our "Services" page for our mail-in form and shipping instructions. We accept all major film formats.

What's the turnaround time?

Standard processing: 3-5 business days. Rush options available for an additional fee.

Can I rent the darkroom?

Yes! Hourly and monthly memberships available. Visit our Rentals page for pricing and availability.

Do you sell film and chemistry?

Absolutely! We stock fresh and expired film from Kodak, Ilford, Fuji, and CineStill in our online store.
