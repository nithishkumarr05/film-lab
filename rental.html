Common Questions

Rental FAQs

Do I need experience to use the darkroom?

Basic darkroom experience is required. We offer orientation sessions for beginners.

Is chemistry included in the rental?

Yes! All chemistry (developer, stop bath, fixer) is included for all rental plans.

How do I get 24/7 access?

Monthly members receive a key-card with 24/7 access. Hourly and day passes are valid during business hours.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, memberships can be cancelled with 30 days notice. No long-term contracts required.
